Questions, answered

Is it really for my kind of work?

It's built for businesses paid per visit: dog walking, dog and cat sitting, house cleaning, gardening and lawn care, handyman work, window cleaning, mobile hairdressing and beauty, and other one-person rounds. If you visit clients and charge per job, it fits.

Where does my data live?

In the demo and the Complete edition, your data stays in your own browser — nothing is uploaded. Take a full backup any time from Settings. The website edition (with accounts, coming at launch) syncs across your devices.

Do I need an account?

No. The demo works right now with no sign-up, no card, no email. Press "Look around with example data" to see a dog-walking business already set up.

Are the tax figures safe to use?

They use 2026/27 rates and allowances for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, including the £1,000 trading allowance and 45p mileage. They're a careful guide, not tax advice — check with HMRC or an accountant before filing.

What does Premium actually add?

Recurring visits that book the next 12 weeks, report cards with photos, receipt photos, household invoicing, cancellations, payment receipts, monthly visit summaries, platform income (Rover, TaskRabbit, Bark) and the box-by-box Self Assessment figures. Everything else is free.
